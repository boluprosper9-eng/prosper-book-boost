# Book Discovery Hub

Build a complete, modern, premium author book-promotion funnel website for my brand:

PROSPER FESTORS

The website should feel like a professional publishing and book-marketing platform. The visual experience can take inspiration from the clean, modern structure of platforms such as STEM.io, but it must be an original design and must NOT copy their branding, logo, proprietary text, or exact layout.

BRAND

Brand name:

PROSPER FESTORS

Create a professional wordmark/logo treatment for the header using the brand name.

Brand positioning:

"Helping authors create stronger opportunities for book discovery, reader engagement, and online visibility."

The brand should feel:

Professional

Established

Trustworthy

Modern

Premium

Author-focused

Technology-driven

FUNNEL STRUCTURE

Build the funnel as:

LANDING PAGE
↓
HOW IT WORKS
↓
BOOK & AUTHOR INFORMATION
↓
PROMOTION OPTIONS
↓
REVIEW REQUEST
↓
SUBMISSION
↓
THANK YOU PAGE

The THANK YOU PAGE is mandatory and must be the final destination after successful submission.

PAGE 1 — HERO / LANDING PAGE

Header:

PROSPER FESTORS

Navigation:

Home
How It Works
Services
About
Contact

Hero headline:

"Help Your Book Get Discovered by More Readers"

Subheadline:

"PROSPER FESTORS helps authors strengthen their Goodreads presence, improve book discoverability, and create professional promotional strategies designed to connect books with relevant readers."

Primary CTA:

"Promote My Book"

Secondary CTA:

"See How It Works"

Add an elegant book/publishing visual.

Below the hero, add a small trust statement:

"Built for authors who want their books to be discovered."

SECTION — WHY GOODREADS?

Headline:

"Why Goodreads Matters for Authors"

Explain that Goodreads is a reader-focused platform where people discover books, track what they want to read, share recommendations, review books, and explore titles based on genres, themes, and reading interests.

Create three cards:

DISCOVERABILITY

"Put your book in front of readers actively looking for their next read."

READER ENGAGEMENT

"Create more opportunities for readers to interact with and discover your book."

BOOK VISIBILITY

"Build a stronger online presence around your title and its target readership."

CTA:

"Improve My Book's Visibility"

PAGE 2 — HOW IT WORKS

Headline:

"How PROSPER FESTORS Works"

Create a 3-step process.

01 — BOOK ASSESSMENT

"We review your book, genre, current online presence, positioning, and potential readership."

02 — VISIBILITY STRATEGY

"We identify relevant opportunities for improving your book's discoverability and reader visibility."

03 — PROMOTIONAL SUPPORT

"We implement the agreed promotional strategy and provide updates throughout the process."

CTA:

"Start My Book Assessment"

Include a small disclaimer:

"Promotional results vary depending on the book, audience, platform activity, and other factors. No specific ranking, review count, or sales result is guaranteed."

Headline:

"Tell PROSPER FESTORS About Your Book"

Subheadline:

"Complete the short form below so we can understand your book and promotional goals."

Fields:

Author Name
Email Address
Book Title
Book Genre
Goodreads Book URL

Book Description

Question:

"What would you most like to achieve?"

Options:

Increase Goodreads visibility

Reach more relevant readers

Promote a new release

Increase reader engagement

Build a stronger author presence

Other

CTA:

"Continue"

Use a multi-step form with clear validation.

Optimize for:

Capture:

Author name

Email

Book title

Genre

Goodreads URL

Amazon URL

Book description

Create the structure so submissions can later be reviewed by the PROSPER FESTORS team.

IMPORTANT FUNNEL BEHAVIOR

The exact user journey must be:

PROSPER FESTORS LANDING PAGE
↓
HOW IT WORKS
↓
AUTHOR & BOOK INFORMATION
↓
PROMOTION SERVICES
↓
REVIEW REQUEST
↓
SUBMIT REQUEST
↓
THANK YOU PAGE

Make the website:

Production-ready

Professional

Conversion-focused

Easy to navigate

Before completing the project, test the complete funnel from the landing page through form submission and verify that the final destination is the PROSPER FESTORS Thank You page.
